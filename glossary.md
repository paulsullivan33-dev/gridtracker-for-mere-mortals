# Glossary

The terms this guide uses, collected in
one place.

**ADIF** — Amateur Data Interchange
Format: the standard text format for
exchanging log records between amateur
radio programs, a list of records made
of named fields such as `<call:5>`.

**Alert** — a rule in GridTracker that
speaks, sounds, or displays when an
incoming report matches something you
asked to be told about.

**Award** — a certificate earned by
confirmed contacts meeting a pattern:
countries (DXCC), US states (WAS),
grid squares (VUCC), zones, and more.
GridTracker counts your progress from
your loaded log.

**Call Roster** — GridTracker's table
window of stations in the current
reports, one row each, coloured by
worked and wanted status.

**Callsign** — a station's licensed
identifier, the letters-and-number name
every report is filed under.

**Datagram** — one self-contained UDP
packet: a GridTracker report travels
as exactly one.

**Decode** — WSJT-X turning a slice of
received audio into a message; also the
report type that carries one decoded
message to GridTracker.

**DXCC** — the country-like "entity" a
callsign belongs to, the unit of the
best-known award; GridTracker resolves
callsigns to entities from its bundled
country data.

**Feed** — an incoming stream of other
people's spots, shown in the Feed
Roster and on the map.

**Grid square** — a Maidenhead locator:
a short code (FN42, EM79) naming a
rectangle of the Earth's surface, the
unit in which digital contacts report
position.

**Greyline** — the moving band of
twilight around the Earth, drawn on the
map as an overlay.

**Heartbeat** — the WSJT-X report type
that simply announces the sender is
alive, with its name and version.

**Logbook view** — GridTracker's
internal picture of your contacts,
built from loaded ADIF files, live
Logged reports, and downloads; not a
master log.

**Lookup** — asking an online service
(Callook, QRZ, and others) what it
knows about one callsign.

**OAMS** — GridTracker's own online
service through which its users spot
and see each other.

**POTA / SOTA** — Parks on the Air and
Summits on the Air: activity programmes
whose activators appear as feeds and
map layers.

**QSO** — one completed contact between
two stations.

**QSL** — a confirmation of a contact;
the QSL counter counts confirmed
records in the log view.

**Spot** — a small public report that
one station heard another, with time
and frequency; PSK Reporter gathers
them, feeds redistribute them.

**Status** — the WSJT-X report type
describing one decoder window whole:
frequency, mode, both stations, both
grids; the report that makes an
instance real to GridTracker.

**UDP** — the connectionless network
protocol WSJT-X reports travel by;
port 2237 is GridTracker's default ear.

**WSJT-X** — the weak-signal decoder
program whose reports are GridTracker's
reason to exist.
